## IX. VALIDATION PIPELINE

`python python/validate.py` — tek giris noktasi.

Kontroller:
1. Schema dosyalarinin varligi ve yapisi (schemas/*.schema.json)
2. Tam 5 track dosyasi (tracks/radical.{grey,blue,green,cream,black}.json)
3. Registry tutarliligi (knowledge/registry.json)
4. Sound library schema ve asset kontrolleri (25 asset, unique id)
5. Track-library binding (her track'in library_binding'i sound_library'deki eslesen color_state asset'lere isaret etmeli)
6. Album duration politikasi (radio max < extended min)
7. jsonschema ile track validation

Exit 0 = PASS, Exit 1 = FAIL + violation listesi.

CI: `.github/workflows/validate.yml` — push/PR'da otomatik calisir.
